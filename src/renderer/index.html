A studio for living daguerreotypes

Long Exposure

In 1840 a portrait took a minute. You had to hold still. Your heart couldn't.

The sitting agreement

  • What the camera reads: the shape of your face, your blinks, whether you are speaking, and your pulse and breathing from tiny changes in your skin. The SDK also reports expression scores; the studio uses only one of them, a held smile, and ignores the rest.
  • What is kept: no photographs or video, ever. Your plate is drawn from face geometry and rhythm, and it lives in memory until you export it or leave.
  • Only you: the sitting stops if a second face appears, and the lens caps whenever you talk, move or switch away from this window.
  • Network: this app sends nothing itself. Presage telemetry is off. The SDK contacts Presage to check the API key and count usage (Presage's privacy notes).
  • Not medical: this is portraiture. It makes no health claims and should not be used for any.

Sit square to the camera, face a lamp or window, and keep the camera still. The sitting takes about a minute of stillness.
