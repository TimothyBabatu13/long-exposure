# Demo video storyboard (about 2:30)

Film it split-screen: the real sitter on a phone camera on the left, the screen on the right. A fingertip oximeter in frame is optional, as a sync reference only. Caption it "reference, not a medical claim". Keep one **unedited** 90-second take in the repo or linked from it, so judges can check that nothing was faked.

| Time | Shot | Proves |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Black plate, ticking clock. Title card: *"In 1840 a portrait took a minute. You had to hold still. Your heart couldn't."* | Hook |
| 0:10 | The sitting agreement. Press Enter. The Studio Ledger reads 0 frames saved, 0 requests, telemetry off | Consent and data |
| 0:25 | The ghost portrait emerges. The sitter blinks and ghost eyes appear, logged in the ledger | Real-time face signals |
| 0:40 | At about 12 s the first flush, with the heartbeat sound. The oximeter beep lines up, or at least its rate does | Live pulse |
| 0:55 | The sitter speaks and the lens caps: "Hold still… no talking". Then they fall silent and it uncaps | Talking signal, honest limits |
| 1:10 | At about 30 s the bust starts breathing. The sitter's real chest is visible on the left | Breathing |
| 1:30 | At about 60 s the seal is engraved. The plate flips to the verso: waveform, readouts, beat tier, cause log | Evidence |
| 1:50 | **Perturbation proof:** a hand over the face caps the lens; a second sitter, or the same sitter after 20 squats (disclosed), shows a faster beat; Double Exposure puts the two plates side by side in different rhythms. *"It's not a filter. It's them."* | It's real |
| 2:15 | A bad-light attempt shows "The light is wrong…", then gentle mode, then **Leave the studio** wipes everything | Graceful failure, control |
| 2:30 | *"Sit for a plate. Post yours in the comments."* The GIF in a DEV comment | Call to action |
